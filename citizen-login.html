CyberCouncil Terms & Privacy

CyberCouncil is an educational and public awareness cybersecurity initiative. In this evaluation release:

1. Privacy: We never transmit, store, or sell personal passwords or sensitive data on remote servers. All credential checks on this page run strictly client-side.

2. Ethical Use: Tools such as phishing scanners and file metadata viewers are designed solely for self-defense, educational auditing, and authorized digital safety verification.

3. Incident Reporting: Incident records generated within the platform are held in localized browser state during evaluation and are not submitted to live police databases.
