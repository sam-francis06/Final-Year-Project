Electronic & Digital Records Evidentiary Framework BSA 2023 • Section 63

Electronic and digital records have specific evidentiary considerations under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (replacing Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872). Digital files, server logs, mobile extractions, and electronic messages are not automatically admissible without satisfying statutory conditions regarding source integrity, lawful custody, device control, and proper certificate verification under Section 63.
