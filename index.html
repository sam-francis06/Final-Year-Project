Key Features

A unified architecture bridging citizen self-defense with specialized law enforcement capabilities.

Cybersecurity Tools

Essential defensive utilities empowering individuals to assess digital risks and protect personal assets.

  • Phishing detection & suspicious link scanner
  • Password auditing & entropy analyzer
  • Privacy risk & permission analyzer
  • File metadata inspection & threat detection

Incident Reporting

Structured reporting workflow for citizens to document and log cyber offenses with evidence.

  • Report financial fraud, identity theft & harassment
  • Track submitted reports with generated tracking IDs
  • Maintain an organized personal incident history log
  • Attach digital evidence files and transaction proofs

Cybercrime Investigation

Operational workspace for law enforcement officers to conduct technical investigations.

  • Full-cycle case management & status progression
  • Digital forensic utilities & artifact extraction
  • Cross-platform username & OSINT investigation
  • URL intelligence, live status & WHOIS lookup

Forensics & Evidence

Technical verification utilities ensuring cryptographic integrity and evidence preservation.

  • Cryptographic hash generation (MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256)
  • EXIF camera metadata & GPS geodata extraction
  • File MIME signature validation against spoofed extensions
  • Client IP detection & network diagnostic tools

Crime Hotspot Mapping

Spatial intelligence visualization designed to uncover geographic patterns in cybercrime.

  • Visualize aggregated cybercrime case locations
  • Identify geographic patterns & clusters from reports
  • Interactive map markers with severity categorization
  • Jurisdictional trend analysis for resource planning

Cyber Law Reference

Comprehensive legal guide covering the Indian IT Act and procedural standards for cyber offenses.

  • Citizen-facing statutory guidance and rights
  • Police investigation legal reference & section codes
  • Penalty schedules, bailable status & precedents
  • Real-world case studies & legal scenario mapping

About CyberCouncil

CyberCouncil is a unified cybersecurity and cybercrime response platform designed to connect citizen reporting with police investigation workflows.

Citizen Portal

Public Awareness & Defense

Empowers everyday internet users with proactive threat detection tools, legal literacy, and transparent incident reporting channels.

  • Awareness: Understand evolving cyber threat vectors, social engineering, and fraud mechanisms.
  • Security Inspection Tools: Run self-service scans for phishing links, password strength, and app permissions.
  • Incident Reporting: Guided reporting interface to submit complaints with relevant transaction or file evidence.
  • Incident Tracking: Check status updates and review historical incident records in a private console.
  • Cyber-Law Awareness: Plain-language breakdowns of rights and penalties under the Indian Information Technology Act.
Police Console

Law Enforcement & Investigation

Equips law enforcement agencies, cyber cells, and investigators with technical forensic tools, hotspot intelligence, and case tracking.

  • Case Management: Structured case tracking with urgency metrics, status timelines, and officer assignment.
  • Digital Investigation Tools: OSINT username tracking, URL status pinging, WHOIS registration, and screenshot audits.
  • Evidence Analysis: File checksum generation (SHA-256), EXIF location extraction, and MIME type verification.
  • Crime Hotspot Mapping: Spatial analysis of incident coordinates to detect geographic crime clusters.
  • Cyber-Law Reference: Legal handbook mapping specific digital crimes to corresponding IT Act sections and court precedents.

How CyberCouncil Works

Bridging the gap between citizen incident reporting and specialized law enforcement response.

1

Citizen

Citizen encounters a digital threat or undergoes a cyber incident.

2

Report Incident

Incident details and evidence artifacts are submitted through the portal.

3

Case Database

Standardized intake and staging for jurisdictional review and triage.

4

Police Case Management

Officers assess priority, verify reports, and assign cases to investigation teams.

5

Investigation

Specialized forensic verification, URL intelligence, and OSINT tracing.

6

Resolution & Updates

Case status milestones recorded and preventative insights visualized on hotspot maps.

Implementation Notice: End-to-end cloud database integration linking citizen incident reporting with police case management represents the platform's multi-phase deployment roadmap. Currently, modules operate on localized frontend states and mock datasets for evaluation and demonstration.
